# StegoForge Legal & Ethical Use Notice

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-23  

---

## 1. Authorized Use Policy

StegoForge is a data confidentiality and concealment engine designed exclusively for:
- Personal privacy protection and secure data storage
- Authorized penetration testing and security research engagements
- Academic study and cryptography/steganography education
- Digital forensics and blue-team detection tooling evaluation

## 2. Operator Responsibility

Steganography and cryptographic software are subject to differing laws and export regulations across jurisdictions. The operator of this software is solely responsible for ensuring compliance with:
- All applicable national, federal, state, and local laws
- Organizational policies, non-disclosure agreements, and terms of service
- Explicit written authorization before conducting penetration tests or covert channel demonstrations on client systems

## 3. Anti-Malware / AV-Evasion Boundary (Non-Negotiable)

In strict adherence to **Architecture Decision ADR-005**:
- StegoForge will never include features designed primarily to evade antivirus (AV), endpoint detection and response (EDR), or data loss prevention (DLP) systems.
- StegoForge is an auditable tool for confidential data concealment, not a malware payload delivery framework.
- The `analyze` command is explicitly provided to aid in identifying StegoForge payload structures for forensic and defensive research.
